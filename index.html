    How it works

    1. You say what you likeLove counts the full price, Maybe about a third, Nope nothing. A heart makes an item count 3×.
    2. We read the shopWhat is in today's shop, when each item leaves, and what new stuff usually shows up on each weekday.
    3. We roll the diceNobody knows tomorrow's shop. So we play 400 pretend futures and see which start day grabs the most of what you like.
    4. X marks your dayThe day with the most loot is your top day. "45% sure" means it landed within 5% of the best start day in 45 of every 100 pretend futures.

    Why it works

    What "% sure" means

    Try it: roll 20 pretend futures

    Each coin is one pretend future. It lands on the start day that won it.

    It is a smart guess from past shops, not a promise: Epic can change the shop at any time.
